London Weather Art

Weather as Canvas

Every four hours, London's weather becomes art. Real-time temperature, humidity, wind, and sky conditions feed into an AI that paints what the city feels like — not what it looks like. Fourteen art styles rotate through the days: sumi-e ink wash, art deco geometry, cyberpunk neon, stained glass light, and more. No two pieces are alike.
